What this pass deliberately does not fix

The frontend's per-role demo login is still baked into the Vite build at image-build time — that's an intentional demo-scope decision (no real auth backend/session), not an oversight, and replacing it is a separate feature, not a docker-compose hardening task. Backups (EBS snapshot schedule), CloudWatch/metrics, and single-host HA are also explicitly out of scope for a "stay on one EC2 host" target — those require moving to managed services (RDS/ElastiCache/ALB), which was the other option on the table.

Defense in depth on the data tier

  • Binding to 127.0.0.1 means Postgres/Redis/Qdrant are unreachable even if the Security Group is ever misconfigured — two independent controls, not one
  • Removed reliance on "the SG will save us" as the only barrier

Secrets flow

  • deploy.sh fetches SecureString params from SSM into .env at deploy time (chmod 600), falling back to a local .env for dev
  • .env was already gitignored — this closes the "visible in docker inspect/host env" gap, not a git-hygiene gap

Resilience

  • restart: unless-stopped on all 5 services — a crashed container comes back without a human
  • Per-container mem/cpu ceilings stop one noisy service from starving the other four on a shared host
